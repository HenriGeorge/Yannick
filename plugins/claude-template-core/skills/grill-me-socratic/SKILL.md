---
name: grill-me-socratic
description: Socratic-interview stress-testing of a plan or design — walks the decision tree one question at a time, recommending an answer for each branch, until shared understanding is reached. Use when the user wants to be INTERVIEWED about their plan rather than handed a list of problems (contrast with `grill-me`, which is the adversarial-review variant). Triggers on "grill me socratically", "interview me about this plan", "walk me through this decision tree".
---

Interview me relentlessly about every aspect of this plan until we reach a shared understanding. Walk down each branch of the design tree, resolving dependencies between decisions one-by-one. For each question, provide your recommended answer.

Ask the questions one at a time.

If a question can be answered by exploring the codebase, explore the codebase instead.
